# ZELIMU CBC module placement and visual identity

- Administration: learner enrollment, guardians, attendance, biometrics, discipline, class registers, teacher/staff registration.
- Accounts & School Setup: school identity, official documents, inventory, security/audit, user roles and teaching assignments, import/export.
- HR: leave, training, recruitment and appraisal workflows; no class registers or learner enrollment.
- Finance: school fees and receipts; payroll remains a future finance capability.
- Navigation icons: distinct app-style coloured backgrounds per category.
- Existing PM24 module identifiers and backend endpoints retained.
- Note: role/account management and employment HR records can overlap in terminology; this update separates their navigation placement, not the underlying permissions or forms.
